Silver Anniversary • Family Day Plan

25 Years, One Sunrise, One Blessing, One Match

A warm anniversary day starting before sunrise from Pandeypur, reaching Chopan Son River, taking blessings at Bhandari Mai Ahraura, returning home for underhand cricket, cake cutting and a batti chokha dinner.

Open Maps Page

Start

2:15 AM

Leave Pandeypur, Varanasi. Keep fuel, water, torch, power bank and light snacks ready before sleeping.

Sunrise

5:15–5:25 AM

Reach Chopan Son River / old bridge area before first light. Stay away from the river edge in darkness.

Finish

8:00 PM onward

End the day with family, cake, memories and a proper batti chokha dinner at home.

Silver Anniversary Underhand Cricket Cup

Match format 5 overs per side, soft tennis ball or plastic ball, everyone gets to bat.

  • Teams: Team Husband vs Team Wife, or Team 25 Years vs Team Forever.
  • Only underhand bowling.
  • One-tip-one-hand catch can be out.
  • Anniversary couple gets 25 bonus runs.
  • Final over is called The Silver Over.
  • No hard hitting near windows or parked vehicles.

Things to keep ready

  • Soft ball
  • Plastic bat
  • Chalk or tape for crease
  • Water and chairs
  • Phone tripod for video

Batti Chokha Dinner

Dinner title Silver Anniversary Bhoj: Batti Chokha Night

  • Batti with ghee
  • Baingan chokha
  • Aloo chokha
  • Tomato chutney and green chutney
  • Salad: onion, lemon, green chilli, cucumber
  • Dahi or raita, papad, pickle
  • Sweet ending: gur or kheer
